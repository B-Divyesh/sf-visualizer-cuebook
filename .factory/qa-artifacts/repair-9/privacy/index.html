Privacy

Your track and set remain on your device. Cuebook has no accounts and does not upload your creative work.

What is stored

Cuebook stores one track and its set in this browser. Use Start a new set, site-data controls, or cue-file export to manage that data.

What leaves your device

Cuebook sends no track, set, or usage data to another service. Your browser requests only the files needed to run this site.

Analytics and audio

Cuebook has no analytics or trackers. It loads no scripts or fonts from other sites. Your track is never included in a cue-file export.

Your controls

  • Export a cue file from the cue sheet.
  • Use Start a new set to delete the active track and set.
  • Use browser site-data controls to remove the saved set, audio, and cached app files.
