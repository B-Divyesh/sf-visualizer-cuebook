# Cuebook visual thesis

## Direction: luminous glass data landscape

Cuebook should feel like a private rehearsal desk after the room lights go down: precise time markings suspended over deep, translucent space. It is not an effect gallery. The live image is the content; controls recede into smoked glass and the cue rail reads like a scored performance. The landscape metaphor appears as a low horizon, time contours, and bright “beacons” at saved transitions.

## Palette

This is an explicitly dark, performance-room interface. A light theme would produce glare during rehearsal and weaken the projected-color preview, so the product paints every route and install splash dark.

| Token | Value | Role |

| Night | `#07110f` | Page and install background |
| Basin | `#0c1b18` | Raised workspace |
| Glass | `rgba(18, 42, 37, .76)` | Controls floating over the landscape |

Color never carries meaning alone: active states include labels/shapes; notices include icons and text.

## Typography and spacing

- **Display:** self-hosted “Space Grotesk” subset (OFL), 600. Its squared bowls and open counters suit a timing instrument without turning into sci-fi pastiche.
- **Utility/body:** system UI stack. Small timing values use tabular numerals. Body copy is at least 16 px, with a 1.5 line height.
- Type scale: 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 28 / clamp(40–64) px. The 12/14 sizes are labels only, never essential body instructions.
- Spacing follows a 4 px base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Main gutters are 16 px on phones and 32 px above 760 px.
- Corners are restrained: 8 px on controls, 16 px on large glass planes. Thin luminous edges describe depth; shadows do not simulate generic cards.

## Layout and interaction grammar

The first-use screen pairs a concise promise with an original glass topography. After import, the canvas becomes the dominant object. The transport and cue rail sit directly below it; scene controls and the ordered cue sheet follow. Adding a cue always captures the audio element’s authoritative `currentTime`, then annotates the calculated beat—analysis never decides timing. Selecting a cue seeks to it. Space toggles playback when focus is not inside a form field; `M` adds a marker.

On phones the artwork is dropped after import, the transport stacks, cue rows become two-line blocks, and nonessential explanations collapse. Touch targets remain at least 44 px.

## Motion policy

Interface transitions use 180–240 ms opacity and transform changes. The scene canvas is animated only while audio plays; when paused it resolves a deterministic still for the exact audio timestamp. Nothing decorative loops. With `prefers-reduced-motion`, interface movement becomes immediate opacity changes and canvas motion uses stepped, low-amplitude state changes. No flash exceeds 3 Hz.
