Privacy, in plain language.

Your audio, cue sheets, and rehearsal recordings remain on your device. Cuebook has no account system and does not upload your creative work.

What is stored

The installed app stores your current audio file and cue project in your browser’s IndexedDB. A purchased license token and its last verification result are stored in localStorage. You can remove project data with “New set,” remove all data through browser settings, and export cue timing as JSON at any time.

What leaves your device

Only license verification contacts the Sociobot billing API when you restore or use Cuebook Plus. It sends the license token needed to confirm the purchase. Checkout is hosted by Sociobot, with Dodo as merchant of record; their checkout privacy terms apply there.

Analytics and audio

Cuebook includes no behavioral analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, cloud audio processing, or third-party runtime scripts. Your audio is never included in cue JSON exports.

Your controls

  • Export cue JSON from the cue sheet.
  • Use “New set” to delete the active project and audio from Cuebook storage.
  • Use browser site-data controls to remove the project, service worker cache, and license.
